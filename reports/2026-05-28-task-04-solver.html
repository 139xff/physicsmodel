Task 04: Electrostatic solver with preview and refined modes

DONE Completed: 2026-05-28T09:35:06+08:00 Review fix updated: 2026-05-28T14:38:00+08:00

Scope Delivered

  • Added a pure Python electrostatic solver package with explicit SI constants epsilon0 and k = 1/(4*pi*epsilon0).
  • Implemented analytic point charge superposition, infinite-plane field with documented potential reference, and spherical-shell inside/outside formulas.
  • Implemented arbitrary-orientation ring evaluation with preview/refined discrete segment counts and axis analytic regression coverage.
  • Implemented baseline numerical midpoint evaluators for line segments and disks, each returning limitation warnings instead of silent precision claims.
  • Bounded singular or near-source point-charge samples to finite zero contribution with per-source and per-sample warnings.
  • Added POST /api/field/evaluate for batch sample evaluation with request_id echo, result_version, quality metadata, per-source contributions, warnings, and solver metadata.
  • Updated /api/config so solver capability is available while scene editing and interaction remain planned.
  • Review fix: ring contributions now warn that they use a finite-segment approximation while preserving discrete-ring metadata.
  • Review fix: ring samples on or extremely near the ring wire and line-segment samples on or extremely near the finite segment interior now emit near-source limitation warnings in both contribution and sample summaries.

Scope Deferred

  • No browser probe UI, overlays, shared 2D/3D state, or Playwright browser interaction was added in this task.
  • Line-segment and disk evaluators are intentionally baseline numerical approximations, not final analytic or high-accuracy quadrature implementations.
  • Infinite-plane potential uses a local zero-at-plane reference because absolute potential for an infinite plane is reference-dependent.

Known Risks And Limitations

  • Point-charge singularities are bounded to zero contribution at or within 1e-9 m and produce explicit warnings, avoiding infinities and NaNs in totals.
  • Ring fields use finite midpoint charge segments; refined mode increases segments but is still a numerical approximation off axis and now reports that limitation in contribution and sample warnings.
  • Line-segment and disk fields use midpoint numerical approximations and return limitations in warnings; they are available for baseline exploration, not final precision claims.
  • Ring and finite-line geometric near-source checks warn at or within 1e-9 m of the ideal continuous geometry, but they do not replace the current finite-segment numerical result with an analytic singular treatment.
  • Infinite-plane potential is reported relative to V=0 at the plane, while the field is analytic away from the discontinuity.
