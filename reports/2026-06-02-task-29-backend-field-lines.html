Known risks or limitations

  • The backend applies source-pair and display caps but does not yet reproduce the browser tracer's full spatial segment-conflict index. Browser migration remains a separate task.
  • The 96-candidate CUDA trace launches one block and triggers a Numba low-occupancy warning. Task 9 will measure CPU/CUDA crossover behavior and tune automatic selection.
  • Only charged point sources seed and terminate backend 2D field lines. Other source kinds still contribute to the traced field.
