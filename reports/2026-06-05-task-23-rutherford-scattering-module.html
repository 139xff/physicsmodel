Scope delivered

Verification evidence

  • The numerical scattering angle matches the Rutherford theory angle within focused tolerances for symmetric impact parameters.
  • The head-on particle turns back at approximately the characteristic distance of closest approach.
  • The browser smoke test renders five scattering tracks, a nucleus marker, five alpha markers, and at least one backscattered track.
  • The existing field-line and motion playback browser tests still pass.

Known risks and limitations

  • The UI uses scaled classroom units, not a literal gold-foil laboratory length and speed scale.
  • The solver uses a fixed time step; very aggressive small-impact-parameter settings may need a smaller dt for tighter angle agreement.
  • The dynamically inserted panel avoids touching legacy mojibake text in web/index.html.
