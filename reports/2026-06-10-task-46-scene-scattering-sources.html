Known Risks and Limitations

  • The current scene-mode scattering angle is derived from the final numeric velocity, so accuracy depends on time step, maximum steps, and backend quality.
  • For multi-source scenes, the displayed closest approach is the minimum geometric distance to any source, not a source-weighted physical cross-section.
  • The browser preserves point-nucleus mode only when the scene has no electrostatic sources.
